Today, I wanted to register a new domain for my homelab services. With registrars steadily raising prices across almost every TLD lately, I searched for the cheapest option (for both registration and renewal) and came across `.xyz`'s 1.111B Class of all-numeric domains via [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/comments/sp8etq/comment/hwdgztx/).

The [1.111B Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.xyz#1.111B_Class) covers every all-numeric domain from `000000.xyz` to `999999999.xyz`, so essentially all 6-, 7-, 8- and 9-digit `.xyz` domains. The registry has (hopefully) committed to keeping these at around a dollar a year, renewals included. I registered mine for 0.85 USD at Namecheap.

Of course, when anyone can register a domain for less than a dollar, the low barrier to entry also attracts malicious actors. Wikipedia even has a [dedicated section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.xyz#Malicious_domains) on the reputation of `.xyz` domains. Since I'm not going to send emails from this domain, I should be mostly fine on that front.

So the next time you're shopping for a domain for your internal services, give `.xyz`'s 1.111B class a try. :)
